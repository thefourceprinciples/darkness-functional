# Glossary

This glossary defines the working vocabulary for the Darkness Functional.

## Darkness Load

A derived diagnostic measure of coherence reduction, noise, constraint failure, and interface mismatch.

## Coherence

The degree to which interacting components maintain stable relationships across time.

## Noise

Variance, perturbation, randomness, or disruptive fluctuation that reduces usable alignment.

## Constraint Failure

Weakening, brittleness, leakage, or collapse of the boundary conditions that help a system preserve structure.

## Interface Mismatch

Misalignment between subsystems, scales, layers, protocols, or boundaries.

## Persistence

A system's ability to maintain recognizable structure across time and perturbation.

## Throughput

The sustaining flow of energy, matter, information, attention, resources, computation, or communication.

## Fertile Darkness

Latent, protected, unexpressed, or not-yet-visible potential. This is not the main target of the functional.

## Coherence-Loss Darkness

Hidden distortion, incoherence, accumulated contradiction, or loss of usable structure. This is the main target of the functional.

## Restoration

Any intervention that improves future coherence, reduces noise, repairs constraints, or realigns interfaces.

## Accumulated Load

Historical coherence-loss burden that may continue to affect a system even after partial restoration.
