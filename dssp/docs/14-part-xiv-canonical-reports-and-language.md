## 111. Canonical advancement statement

Candidate C advanced from state (E_i, X_j) to (E_i+1, X_j) after passing gates G0-G4 under scope S. It remains on HOLD at G5 because no controlled interface has been qualified.

Advancement language always states:

- axis;
- prior state;
- new state;
- passed gates;
- restrictions;
- unresolved gates;
- scope.

## 112. Canonical failure statement

The proposed electromagnetic interface failed G6 because the sham channel reproduced the measured response. The residual remains real as an apparatus behavior but no longer supports the claimed hidden-sector coupling.

Failure must identify what failed without exaggerating the scope.

## 113. Canonical unresolved statement

The residual remains qualified but unresolved. It is currently classified D_U, with evidence level E2 and engineering level X0. No mechanism, interface, or resource claim is authorized.

## 114. Canonical analog statement

The Safe Analog Laboratory recovered a concealed weak coupling under blinded conditions and correctly separated it from tested common-mode and cross-talk pathways. The result qualifies the method within the tested analog domain. It does not establish a dark-sector interaction.

## 115. Canonical stewardship statement

The experiment is qualified at SQ3 within the declared low-energy operating envelope. No dark-sector physical claim, biological exposure, remote scale, commercial transfer, or public application claim is authorized.
