# Final Return Law

Measure the remainder.

Preserve the model that produced it.

Expose the uncertainty surrounding it.

Build the strongest ordinary counterfeit.

Attempt to make the anomaly disappear without making the evidence disappear with it.

Preserve what survives.

Design the test that could defeat it.

Intervene only when causality can be separated from coincidence.

Stop the effect.

Watch what remains.

Close the ledger.

Bound the consequence.

Carry the full load.

Leave more than one future open.

Transfer understanding with capability.

Do not call the work complete until responsibility has somewhere honest to go.

Capability is not permission.

Possession is not understanding.

Control is not containment.

Safety is not stewardship.

Completion is not release.

No darkness is engineered before the claim about it can withstand the light.
