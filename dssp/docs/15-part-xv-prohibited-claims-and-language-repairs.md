# Part XV — Prohibited Claims and Language Repairs

## 116. Prohibited without later gates

- Dark matter was detected.
- The apparatus contains dark energy.
- The Darkness Functional is a physical force.
- The signal proves a hidden dimension.
- The device generates free energy.
- The anomaly can be harvested.
- The effect is safe.
- The effect is contained.
- The mechanism is unique.
- No conventional explanation exists.
- The analog laboratory has contained darkness.

Preferred replacements:

A qualified residual remains after the declared calibration and background treatment.

No tested conventional explanation in the current model basin accounts for the residual within the stated scope.

A novelty candidate is supported under the declared conditions; confirmation remains pending.

The apparatus controls its sensitivity to a proposed interaction. The proposed source is not controlled.

A safe analog result qualifies the method, not the cosmic ontology.

## 117. Corrective language

Artifact found:

The feature was reproduced through the identified apparatus pathway and is classified D_A within the affected channels. This finding does not address unrelated residuals outside that pathway.

Background found:

The event population is consistent with the qualified background model under the current uncertainty range. No target interpretation is supported in this region.

Model failure found:

The observation remains valid, but the residual is removed when the omitted ordinary process is included. The earlier anomaly is reclassified D_M.

Corruption found:

Critical provenance and selection records are incomplete. The physical origin remains unresolved, but the evidence cannot support advancement until the record is repaired.

Compound result:

Calibration drift explains the time-dependent component, while a smaller replicated residual remains. The first component is D_A; the remainder is D_U.
