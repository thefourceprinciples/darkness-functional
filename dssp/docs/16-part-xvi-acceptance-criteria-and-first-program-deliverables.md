# Part XVI — Acceptance Criteria and First Program Deliverables

## 118. Master acceptance criteria

The DSSP foundational architecture is established when:

- dark matter, dark energy, and hidden-sector candidates remain distinct;
- the Darkness Functional remains a residual-analysis framework rather than a physical-force claim;
- every residual is model-indexed;
- raw and transformed data remain separate;
- covariance and uncertainty debt are explicit;
- evidence and engineering maturity remain separate;
- contradictions remain visible;
- no aggregate score overrides a critical failure;
- D_U and D_F remain protected states;
- D_N cannot be assigned automatically;
- simulation, analogy, and physical observation remain typed;
- safe analog qualification precedes exotic intervention;
- false-darkness pathways are actively reproduced;
- energy, momentum, entropy, matter, and lifecycle ledgers are required;
- independent shutdown and release are formal gates;
- Nulls, failures, and rollback remain public scientific assets;
- scale requires new qualification rather than extrapolation;
- stewardship remains appealable, proportionate, and auditable.

## 120. First operational milestone

The first genuine DSSP success is not a dark-sector discovery.

It is a system that is measurably:

- harder to fool;
- harder to corrupt;
- better at preserving unresolved truth;
- safer to perturb;
- capable of rollback;
- and able to release responsibility cleanly.
