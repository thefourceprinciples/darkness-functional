# Part I — Charter and Scientific Boundary

## 1. The dark sector is not one thing

DSSP separates three top-level domains:

DS-M = dark-matter domain

DS-E = dark-energy and cosmic-expansion domain

DS-H = hypothetical hidden-sector domain

Dark matter is the name given to the additional gravitating component or effective structure required by a broad range of astronomical and cosmological observations. Its microscopic identity remains unresolved.

Dark energy is the name given to the cause or effective description of accelerated cosmic expansion. It may be represented by a cosmological constant, a dynamical field, modified gravity, interacting components, or an incomplete model. No local reservoir or technological coupling has been established.

A broader hidden sector may contain proposed dark photons, axion-like particles, sterile or heavy neutral fermions, hidden scalars, composite states, self-interacting particles, or other mediators. These are candidate spaces, not a confirmed inventory.

DSSP therefore enforces:

Dark matter != dark energy != every hidden-sector candidate

Search channel != identified substance

Gravitational inference != captured matter

Cosmological parameter != local engineering medium

## 2. Mission

DSSP exists to:

1. Preserve unexplained physical residuals without prematurely naming them.
2. Separate structured anomaly from artifact, background, fluctuation, model failure, and institutional distortion.
3. Compare ordinary and novel explanations within a common provenance architecture.
4. Map candidate interfaces between observable systems and proposed hidden phenomena.
5. Qualify inference and control methods through safe analog systems before exotic intervention.
6. Require closed accounting before any transduction or resource claim.
7. Establish stewardship and release obligations before controllable effects appear.
8. Preserve contradiction, failed replication, and null results as scientific assets.
9. Maximize validated information per unit of energetic, material, ecological, and human burden.
10. Preserve open futures rather than maximizing immediate extraction.

## 3. Non-mission

DSSP is not presently:

- a dark-matter production program;
- a dark-energy generator;
- a vacuum-energy extraction project;
- a propulsion or gravity-control claim;
- a free-energy program;
- a weapon-development program;
- proof that the Darkness Functional is a physical field;
- proof that Fource is an additional fundamental interaction;
- a substitute for particle physics, cosmology, detector science, statistics, or independent review.

Formal framework != physical mechanism != experimental observation != engineered device

Category collapse is a project-level integrity failure.

## 4. Canonical project law

The Dark-Sector Stewardship Project transforms unexplained remainder into auditable knowledge, then permits engineering only where causality, reversibility, accounting, containment, stewardship, and release have been demonstrated.

Do not extract darkness merely because it answers.

First determine what answered.

Then determine whether the answer survives.

Then determine what the interaction costs.

Then determine whether control can be surrendered as safely as it was acquired.
